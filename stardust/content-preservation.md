# Homepage Content Preservation

- Source: `stardust/current/pages/index.json` and the captured rendered page.
- Headings, offer wording, product/category labels, legal marks, and outbound
  destinations are preserved as captured.
- Tracking-only query parameters are retained where they are part of a captured
  editorial link; opaque anti-bot and telemetry endpoints are not integrations.
- Client-only overlays, ads, consent UI, and personalization are excluded from
  static authored content.
- Source image URLs are temporary pilot references because DA media import is
  deferred. Before production, rehost captured originals in DA and replace URLs.
- Local Tiempos Headline files are fidelity references obtained from the
  authorized source presentation. Confirm production font rights and move them
  through the approved asset process before release.
